Score is probability × impact, each rated 1 to 5. Low 1 to 5, medium 6 to 12, high 15 to 25.

Lessons learned

  • Back up every trained model. The corrupted phishing model was restored in under a day.
  • Check the data set before training starts.
  • Set a target accuracy before training.
